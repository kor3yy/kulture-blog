---

There's a temptation when starting any creative project to begin with everything. Every feature, every tool, every plugin. But the best work often starts with less.

## Constraints breed creativity

When you limit yourself — fewer colors, fewer tools, fewer words — you're forced to make decisions. And decisions are where creativity lives.

## Start with the minimum

Before adding anything, ask:

- Does this serve the core idea?
- Would the project work without it?
- Am I adding this because it's useful, or because it's impressive?

## Remove before you add

It's easier to add to a simple foundation than to simplify a complex one. Start small, ship, and let the project tell you what it needs next.

The art of starting simple isn't about doing less for its own sake. It's about making room for what matters.
