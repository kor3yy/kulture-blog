Leen. My heart, my soul, my everything. This project—every letter, every code, every character typed, every story, every breath, every heartbeat—is dedicated to you. Your presence changed the shape of my life forever, and you're forever going to be that beautiful decorated soul for me.

Your impact is something words will never be able to fully carry. You were, and will always be, the most beautiful soul I have ever known—inside and out. Your passing left a silence that time could never completely fill. Yet somehow, you remain everywhere. In every second, every minute, every hour, every day, every week, and every month that passes, there is a thought of you. I see you in everything beautiful, and find everything beautiful because of you. Your name finds its way into conversations, into memories, and into prayers. You live on through the people who loved you, through the things you inspired, into things that now remind me of you. The ocean, colorful and patterned garments, the color purple, poems, people, and more elegant and pious elements, and through every piece of this project that carries a part of my heart. I love you. Allah yrhamek, habeebit albi. May Allah grant you the highest place in Jannah and surround you with endless mercy and peace my angel.
