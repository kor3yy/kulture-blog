Welcome to **Kulture**! This is the very first post on a brand-new blog about ideas, creativity, and the things that shape our world.

## What to expect

This blog will cover:

- **Art & Design** — what's happening in visual culture
- **Technology** — how tools are changing the way we create
- **Music** — sounds worth your attention
- **Everyday life** — the small moments that make things interesting

## How this site was built

This blog is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted on Cloudflare's global network — which means it loads fast anywhere in the world. It's completely static, so there's no database to manage and no server to maintain.

## What's next

New posts will be added regularly. If you'd like to get in touch, head over to the [About page](/about).

Thanks for reading — and welcome to Kulture.
